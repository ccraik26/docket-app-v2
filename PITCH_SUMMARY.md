# Docket - Pitch Summary

**Docket** is a premium life admin concierge service that helps busy professionals reclaim their time by handling the chaos of bills, insurance, appointments, subscriptions, and administrative tasks through a seamless combination of AI and dedicated human support.

We believe that high-achieving professionals shouldn’t have to spend their weekends buried in paperwork and follow-ups. Docket acts as a trusted “safe harbor” — intelligently managing life’s admin so our clients can focus on what matters most.

### Why Docket?

- **Real Results**: Clients typically save 40–50+ hours per month on administrative work.
- **Premium Experience**: A beautiful, calm platform combined with proactive human concierge support.
- **Production-Ready**: Fully built with real payments (Stripe), email automation (Resend), and secure data (Supabase). Ready to serve paying clients today.

Docket is designed for executives, entrepreneurs, and high-performers who value their time and want a trusted partner to handle the details.
