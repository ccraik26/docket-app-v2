## Immediate Next Steps (First 7 Days)

**Day 1–2**:
- Send welcome message through the platform
- Introduce yourself and confirm you’re their dedicated concierge
- Ask for any urgent items they need handled right away

**Day 3–5**:
- Schedule the **Discovery Call** (30–45 min)
- Review their intake form in detail before the call

**Day 5–7**:
- Complete the Discovery Call
- Build their initial **Custom Support Plan**
- Start taking ownership of 2–3 high-impact tasks

---

## Discovery Call Agenda (Suggested)

1. Welcome & rapport building (5 min)
2. Review their biggest frustrations (10 min)
3. Understand their ideal workflow and communication preferences (10 min)
4. Explain how Docket works and set expectations (10 min)
5. Identify quick wins we can deliver in the first 30 days (5–10 min)
6. Schedule next check-in
