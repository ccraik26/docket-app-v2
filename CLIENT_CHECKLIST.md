# Docket - New Client Quick Start Checklist

**Welcome!** Here’s everything you need to get started quickly.

## ✅ Complete These First

- [ ] Fill out the **Client Intake Form** (link in your welcome email)
- [ ] Add any important upcoming deadlines or renewals
- [ ] Share access to bills, insurance, or service accounts you want us to manage
- [ ] Let us know your preferred communication style

## What Happens Next

1. **Within 24–48 hours**  
   You’ll be assigned a dedicated concierge who will introduce themselves.

2. **Discovery Call**  
   We’ll schedule a relaxed call to understand your needs and priorities.

3. **Custom Plan**  
   We’ll build a personalized system for managing your admin.

4. **Ongoing Support**  
   Message your concierge anytime through the Docket platform. We’ll handle the rest.

## What We’ll Need From You

- Login details for recurring bills and services (we handle this securely)
- Copies of important documents (insurance policies, contracts, etc.)
- Any recurring tasks you want us to fully own

## Our Promise

We move fast, communicate clearly, and treat your time and information with the highest care.

You’re now part of a system designed to give you back dozens of hours every month.
